200,000 jobs through YES

This year marks a milestone of national pride for public-private partnerships. With the support of over 1,900 corporates, the Youth Employment Service has created over 200,000 youth work opportunities across South Africa.

Our 2025 Top 35 Under 35 alumni are testament to this impact. They exemplify the 200,000 youth who have gone through the programme to grow their careers, shift needles in key sectors and start ventures to go from earning to owning. Their stories demonstrate impact multiplied, and futures unlocked.

    Unlocking our economic future

    YES CEO, Ravi Naidoo, explores how South Africa’s youth hold the key to unlocking the country’s economic future. With millions unemployed, he argues that giving youth real opportunities through work and entrepreneurship could transform the economy. Highlighting YES’s success in placing over 200,000 youth Naidoo calls for bold reforms and investment to turn youth potential into sustainable national growth.
